# Pebble Pond

A calm, hand-built nature game that runs in the browser. Shape a mountain brook with stones, logs and earth, let the water pool into a pond, and watch life move in: brook trout, ducks, a heron, deer, a moose, a bear and a busy beaver. Build a log cabin and a settler named Elias will raise it log by log, then fish, cook and sit on his porch as the seasons turn.

**Play:** https://markwalker24-games.github.io/pebble-pond/

## How to play

- **Stones / Logs / Collect:** gather loose stones and fallen logs into your bag, then place them across the stream to build a dam.
- **Dig:** scoop earth and drop it elsewhere. Mud packed onto a dam seals it.
- **Trench:** draw a line from the water and a channel is dug for it to follow.
- **Chop:** fell trees for logs.
- **Build:** a log cabin, then a fishing dock and a fire pit.
- Tap the selected tool again to put it down and move freely around the map.
- Top buttons change the time of day, season and weather. The bookmark saves named ponds.

**Touch:** one finger uses the tool (or drags the map with no tool), two fingers pinch and twist, three fingers tilt.
**Mouse / trackpad:** drag to pan with no tool, Option-drag or right-drag to turn and tilt, pinch or scroll to zoom, arrow keys or WASD to move, Q and E to spin.

## Saving

Progress saves automatically in your browser. Name a pond from the bookmark menu to keep several.

## Tech

A single `index.html` using [three.js](https://threejs.org/) r128 from a CDN. No build step: open the file or serve the folder with any static host.
